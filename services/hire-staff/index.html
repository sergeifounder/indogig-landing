Common questions

What should I send with my enquiry?

Include the venue area, role, headcount, duties, working hours and when you need confirmation. Leave staff documents and bank details out of the first message.

When is the shift covered?

A request starts a conversation. Availability, terms and the assignment need to be confirmed before you count on someone for the shift.

What if we need cover every week?

If you already work with daily staff, discuss bringing them into your own schedule first. For recurring gaps, share the expected days and roles when you enquire.
