Two ways to get started

Invited by your employer?

Open the link from your employer and check the venue and shift details. Ask your manager to resend it if you cannot access it.

Interested in external shifts?

Tell us your area, preferred roles and relevant experience. Opportunities depend on venue requests and availability.

Ask about opportunities

From invitation to payday

01 · See what’s offered

An invitation tells you which venue needs help, what the role is and when the shift starts and ends.

02 · Decide with the details in hand

Check the rate and payment deadline before accepting. If a detail is unclear, ask the venue before you commit.

03 · Record your hours and check payment

Follow the venue’s instructions for attendance and hours. Ask the employer about the agreed payment or any discrepancy.

Before you accept a shift

Review your terms

Check the role, location, hours, rate, who pays and the payment deadline before accepting.

Direct payment and data

Your employer pays you directly. Keep identity documents and bank details out of the initial WhatsApp enquiry.

Common questions

Does an enquiry guarantee a shift?

Available shifts depend on what venues request. A confirmed assignment has its own venue, schedule and pay terms.

Who do I contact if my invitation doesn’t open?

Ask the manager who invited you. They can confirm whether the invitation is current and resend it if needed.
