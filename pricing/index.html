Three separate items

Pilot quote

We scope the fixed pilot fee around one venue, assisted setup, team size and the agreed support.

External sourcing quote

If you request outside cover, agree the service scope and fee before sourcing starts.

Worker pay

The employer agrees and pays wages directly. Scheduling someone from your own team carries no automatic sourcing fee.

Common questions

What goes into a pilot quote?

Agree on the venue, team size, setup help, dates and support before deciding whether to proceed.

Do we pay a sourcing fee for our own team?

Scheduling regular staff or a known daily worker does not trigger a sourcing fee. Outside sourcing is a separate request and quote.

Who pays the worker?

The employer agrees the rate and deadline with the worker and pays them directly. IndoGig quotes its services separately.
