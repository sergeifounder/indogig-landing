About IndoGig

Built in Bali for hospitality teams.

IndoGig is operated by PT INDOGIG HRTECH INDONESIA. Our focus is helping venue managers coordinate the people they already rely on and see where they need extra cover.

How we approach workforce planning

Start with the venue’s own team

Keep regular staff and known daily workers on the same roster. External sourcing is a separate, agreed service.

Use familiar channels

The pilot combines WhatsApp updates with a web workspace for scheduling and hours review.

Keep responsibilities clear

The employer keeps its worker relationships and pays its workers directly. IndoGig scopes and quotes its own services.

Why start with hospitality in Bali?

Different peaks, same planning problem

A restaurant dinner, resort banquet and busy beach-club weekend have different roles and hours. In each case, someone needs to know which shifts are covered before guests arrive.

Build around everyday work

The pilot starts with one manager, one venue and a real weekly schedule. We would review the time spent coordinating staff, confirmed coverage and hours handed over to finance.
