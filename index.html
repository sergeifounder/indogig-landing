A Saturday shift, from gap to plan

Here’s what the proposed workflow looks like for a restaurant manager.

  1. See the gap

    Dinner service needs four people. Two are confirmed for service, while bar and kitchen still need coverage.

  2. Ask the people you know

    Send an invitation to a daily worker and see whether they confirm. For the remaining gap, discuss outside help if needed.

  3. Close the shift

    After service, review the hours and pass the approved record to finance.

Common questions

Who is the pilot for?

Bali hospitality venues with an existing team and recurring shift coordination work.

Who pays workers?

The employer pays workers directly under the agreed terms. IndoGig quotes its services separately.

Can we request external help?

Yes, as a separate enquiry. Availability and commercial terms are confirmed before sourcing.

Do workers need to install an app?

The planned pilot uses mobile web links for worker actions. The manager uses the web workspace for scheduling and reviewing hours.

Can I start with a single venue?

Yes. The proposed 30-day pilot is scoped for one venue and one manager with an existing team and a recurring scheduling problem.
