Hotel staffing example: housekeeping and a banquet

Plan around departures and arrivals

A hotel expects a busy checkout and a banquet on Saturday. Housekeeping and banquet service have different hours and each needs its own confirmed headcount.

Fill the banquet gap

The regular team covers housekeeping. The banquet manager invites a daily worker familiar with the venue and checks their confirmation before the event. If the gap remains, the hotel can enquire about external staff.

Review both shifts

After service, supervisors check their teams’ recorded hours. Approved time records go to the hotel’s existing finance process.
