What we would set up together

Team and templates

Start with around 20–50 existing staff and flex relationships, subject to fit. Review the import before sending invitations.

Roster and coverage

Set up roles and templates, publish a roster and invite your own pool. Review gaps and exceptions with guided support.

Attendance and hours

Review check-in and check-out, resolve discrepancies and hand approved hours to finance.

What we would measure

Weekly operations

Compare manager time, confirmed coverage by cutoff, attendance and time to resolve gaps with your baseline.

End-of-pilot review

Review approved hours, support needs and whether the process is worth continuing for your venue.
